We have arrived at the point where we feel comfortable releasing this to the public. Our version of the Snowball Fight has been worked on for many years and we are proud to showcase what we have. Any suggestions or tips are highly appreciated to making this even better for the community.

This is the |DOS| Snowball Fight mod for the |DOS| 2017-2018 Holiday Bash III. The snowball mod, added maps, and skins were created by many great and talented modders that came long before us. For many years, we have internally compiled this mod to add maps and skins for the fun and enjoyment of our gaming clan. Now we want to share this with all MOHAA players. We strive to make additions and improvements to the mod where possible. We are focusing on getting this mod working perfect in base MOHAA. We are not experts, but will do our best! We give full creative kudos to JV_Map for developing the underpinning of the snowball contest and all the scripting to make it happen.

To install please place the file:

zzzdossnowballfight1.294beta1.pk3

Into your MOHAA/main directory of Medal of Honor Allied Assault. All mods and skins have been checked to be compatible with MOHAA. Remove old versions of these mods from MOHAA/main.
